ROUND 3 · FACETED PLATES · STATIC BY DEFAULT

The 2c direction, angular and standing still.

Every form is now built from straight segments and shaded by clipping it into flat bands along the light axis. That replaces round 2's per-form gradients: it reads more angular, more sculpted, and costs a third as much, because there are no gradient definitions and no filters anywhere in the sprite.

Nothing animates. The previous sheet ran an animation per body group per sprite, which is what made it heavy — twenty-odd sprites each driving eleven compositor layers. Motion is now a single opt-in idle on the whole sprite, off by default. The art carries the state instead of the movement.

Still fully generative. The same six signals drive facet contrast, seam light, mane fullness and droop, muscle definition, claw extension, tail fur, paint coverage, plant species, gourd fill, jewellery count and the smile. Move any slider and all four redraw.
