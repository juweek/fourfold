FOURFOLD
GENERATIVE CREATURE ART · ROUNDS 1–2

Four ways to draw a party that knows how your month went.

All four directions render from one shared skeleton per creature — a small set of primitives plus one procedural signature per character. Nothing is hand-traced. The detail is emitted by code, which is what makes it generative and what lets the same file scale from 42px in a card to 240px in the garden.

Raw history goes in; codified drawing properties come out. Six signals — momentum, vitality, volatility, streak, days idle, level — resolve into thirteen properties that every renderer reads. All four cards below are driven by the same numbers, so what you are comparing is rendering, not data.

Each signature element plots real history. The Spellcaster's constellation is one star per task closed this week. The Summoner's plants are one per streak day, its tail volume is 30-day XP. The Warrior's mane count and length are 60-day mass. The Scholar's ledger line is the actual 30-day score trend. The art is the chart.

ROUND 2

Anime rendering, biotech surface, new cast

The anatomy engine is rebuilt underneath all four. Silhouettes are smooth curves through sampled radii rather than stacked ellipses; hair, limbs, fronds, feathers and antennae are tapered ribbons with a real width profile; eyes are proper anime eyes with iris banding, twin highlights and a weighted lid. That alone is most of the jump from "cute vector" to "illustrated".

Shading is now a hard-edged cel terminator computed per form plus a rim light on the lit arc — the Pokémon key-art read. Biotech comes from bioluminescent nodes, translucent membranes, vein networks and spores, all data-driven. The four cards differ only in surface treatment; the creatures underneath are identical.

Cast: Moth Archivist (Work — data written into wing eyespots) · Squirrel Summoner (paint-dipped tail, four plant species, moss) · Lion Warrior (Blanka build, wild multi-hue mane) · Bird Scholar · Panda Bard (Enjoyment — jewellery, smile, aura). 2a

Cel Bloom

anime cel shading · rim light · bloom

Flat base, one hard cel shadow per form, a hot rim light along the lit edge, and blurred bloom on anything emissive. This is the cleanest anime read and the cheapest of the four to run.

Round 1's ink, now over real anatomy: a weighted outline on every form, cross-hatching confined to the cel shadow instead of the whole shape, translucent wing membranes, and bioluminescent spores. Closest to Scavengers Reign on paper.

Chitin & Glass

material study · sheen · glowing seams

Every form becomes a plate with its own light-to-dark gradient, a soft occlusion shadow, and a two-pass seam light: a blurred coloured glow under a hard white specular. Wings and membranes go translucent. The most rendered, most alien of the four.

The creature is not drawn, it is assembled: points sampled from the same skeleton, one per unit of work, linked along the silhouette. Dormancy scatters the cloud; momentum tightens and quickens it. Literal, and it moves like nothing else here.
